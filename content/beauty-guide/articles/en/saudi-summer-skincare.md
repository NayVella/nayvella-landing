Saudi summer conditions can expose skin to very different environments within the same day: intense outdoor heat followed by extended time in air-conditioned spaces.

Heat and perspiration may make heavy textures feel less comfortable, while dry indoor air can leave some skin feeling tighter.

Avoid responding by automatically over-cleansing.

Choose comfortable hydration, maintain appropriate daytime sun protection and adjust textures according to how your skin responds.

See [**How to Choose the Right Sunscreen**](guide:NVBG-003) and [**How to Identify Your Skin Type**](guide:NVBG-001) for the underlying principles.
