Lower humidity and dry air can leave some skin feeling tighter or less comfortable.

Rather than replacing every product, identify what has actually changed.

Consider whether cleansing feels too aggressive, whether hydration needs have increased, and whether a richer texture feels more comfortable.

Very hot water and unnecessarily harsh cleansing may worsen the sensation of dryness for some people.

Cooler weather does not eliminate ultraviolet exposure, so appropriate daytime sun protection remains relevant.

Make gradual changes according to your skin rather than rebuilding the entire routine each season.
