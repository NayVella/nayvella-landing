Choosing sunscreen involves more than looking at the SPF number. Protection range, formulation, correct use and whether the product fits comfortably into your routine all matter.

## Understanding SPF

SPF describes protection against UVB under the product’s specified test and use conditions.

Look beyond the number and consider **broad-spectrum protection**, covering UVA and UVB as defined by applicable product requirements.

## What SPF Should You Choose?

Many health and dermatology authorities generally recommend broad-spectrum **SPF 30 or higher** for everyday sun protection.

Correct application and reapplication when required remain important.

## Choose a Formulation You Will Actually Use

Consider comfort on your skin, compatibility with your routine, ability to use it as directed and whether the texture encourages consistent use.

## What About Makeup With SPF?

An SPF claim in makeup does not necessarily mean the amount normally applied will provide the labelled level of protection. Do not automatically assume makeup alone replaces dedicated sun protection.

## Reapplication

Follow the product directions. General guidance commonly recommends reapplying about every two hours during ongoing sun exposure and more frequently after swimming or sweating, according to the product instructions.

Water resistance does not mean unlimited protection.

## Sun Protection Is Broader Than Sunscreen

Shade, clothing, sunglasses and other appropriate measures can also help reduce exposure depending on the circumstances.

## Summary

Consider protection level, broad-spectrum coverage, formulation and correct use together.
