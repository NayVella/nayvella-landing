Ramadan can change sleep patterns, daily schedules and the times at which skincare feels convenient.

That does not mean the skin automatically requires an entirely different set of products.

Keep the fundamentals manageable: suitable cleansing, hydration and appropriate daytime sun protection.

In the evening, remove makeup, sunscreen and daily buildup as needed and continue products already known to suit your skin.

Avoid attributing every temporary skin change to fasting alone. Environment, sleep, weather, products and individual factors can all play a role.

General hydration is important for health, but drinking additional water should not be presented as a direct treatment for every skin concern.

The practical objective during Ramadan is **consistency and simplicity**, not a longer routine.
