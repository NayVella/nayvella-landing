Oily skin does not need to be constantly stripped of oil. A useful routine focuses on cleansing, hydration and daytime sun protection while remaining comfortable enough to follow consistently.

## Why Does Skin Become Oily?

Sebum is naturally produced by the skin. When its presence is more noticeable, the face may develop visible shine, particularly around the forehead, nose and chin.

If you are unsure about your skin type, begin with [**How to Identify Your Skin Type**](guide:NVBG-001).

The objective is not to remove every trace of oil, but to maintain comfortable, balanced care.

## Morning Routine

### 1. Cleanse Gently
Choose a daily cleanser that removes excess oil and impurities without leaving the skin noticeably tight or uncomfortable.

### 2. Add Only Useful Steps
A routine does not need many products to be effective. Add products such as serums when they serve a clear purpose. See [**Serum vs Moisturizer vs Toner**](guide:NVBG-012).

### 3. Moisturize
Oily skin can still benefit from moisturizer. Choose a texture that feels comfortable on your skin.

### 4. Use Sun Protection
Sun protection is an important part of daytime skincare. See [**How to Choose the Right Sunscreen**](guide:NVBG-003).

## Evening Routine

Remove makeup and sunscreen appropriately, cleanse gently, introduce additional care products gradually when needed, and finish with suitable hydration.

## Common Mistakes

- Over-cleansing.
- Using harsh products solely for a stronger “clean” feeling.
- Avoiding moisturizer because the skin is oily.
- Introducing several new products at once.
- Selecting products only from an “oily skin” label.
- Neglecting sun protection.

## Does Shine Always Mean Oily Skin?

No. Heat, humidity, physical activity and products can influence how the skin looks during the day.

## When to Seek Professional Assessment

Persistent inflammation, pain, significant irritation, rash or other concerning skin changes are better assessed by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

## Summary

A useful oily-skin routine can remain simple: gentle cleansing, appropriate hydration and daytime sun protection, with additional steps introduced only when needed.
