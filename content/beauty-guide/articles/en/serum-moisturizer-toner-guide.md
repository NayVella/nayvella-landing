These product categories are not interchangeable.

**Moisturizers** generally help maintain skin hydration and comfort.

**Serums** describe a broad formulation category often used to deliver particular ingredients, but the word “serum” alone does not tell you its purpose.

**Toners** vary considerably, so their function depends on the specific formulation.

You do not automatically need all three.

A simple routine built around cleansing, appropriate hydration and daytime sun protection can provide a foundation, with additional products introduced for clear purposes.

Rather than asking which category is “best,” ask what function your routine actually needs.
