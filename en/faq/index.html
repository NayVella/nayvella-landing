Frequently Asked Questions

Effective: 26 July 2026 · Version 2.0

Answers about the current pre-launch website, expressions of interest, privacy, security, and what NayVella does not yet provide.

What is NayVella today?

NayVella is currently a pre-launch informational website operated by Meridian Glow Company. The public forms register interest only.

Does submitting a form create an account or acceptance?

No. A submission does not create an account, booking, order, partnership, professional listing, or binding commercial relationship.

Who can register interest?

Adults may register interest as customers, merchants, clinics or doctors, or beauty specialists using the relevant form.

Does NayVella provide medical diagnosis or treatment?

No. Website information and descriptions of planned smart tools are general information and do not replace advice from a licensed healthcare professional.

How is form information protected?

Forms require consent and use server-side validation, Cloudflare Turnstile, deduplication, rate limiting, encrypted transport, and a separate database binding.

Can I request access, correction, or deletion?

You may submit a privacy request to hello@meridianglow.sa. Applicable requests are handled under Saudi data-protection law and the Privacy Policy.

Are clinics, doctors, and specialists part of NayVella already?

No provider is automatically accepted or activated by submitting a form. Any future participation requires review and separate acceptance.

Where can I read the governing documents?

The current Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Trust Center are linked in the footer of every public page.

NayVella is currently in development and preparing for launch. Registering interest does not create an account, acceptance, or a commercial relationship.

NayVella is a technology platform. Beauty and skincare information on this website is general information and is not medical diagnosis or treatment.
