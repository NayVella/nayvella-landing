Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Meridian Glow Company ("the Company"), owner and operator of the NayVella platform, respects the privacy of this website's visitors and is committed to protecting their personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This policy explains how your data is collected and used during the pre-launch phase.

1. Data we collect

  • Interest-registration data you voluntarily submit through our forms, such as: name, email, mobile number (partner forms), city, and partner business details (business name and type, product or service categories, professional links).
  • General technical data when browsing, such as device type, browser, and traffic source.

At this stage we do not request official documents, personal photos, sensitive data, or passwords.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you regarding the platform launch and related services.
  • To review partner interest requests and prepare for the qualification phase.
  • To improve the website experience and measure campaign effectiveness.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Legal basis

We process your data based on your explicit consent at registration, which you may withdraw at any time.

4. Data sharing

We may engage trusted service providers (such as hosting and email services) to process data on our behalf, strictly as needed, under obligations to protect it and to comply with the law — including its cross-border data transfer provisions where applicable.

5. Data retention

We retain data for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes above, or until you withdraw consent or request deletion, unless the law requires otherwise.

6. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to: be informed of how your data is processed, request access to it or a copy of it, request correction or updating, request destruction, and withdraw your consent to communications at any time. To exercise any right, contact us at the email below. Where applicable, you may lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

7. Minors

This website is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data; any such data identified will be deleted.

8. Data security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access or use.

9. Cookies

The website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to improve the experience and understand traffic sources; you can set your browser to refuse them.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; updates will be posted on this page with their date.

11. Contact

For any privacy inquiry or request, email us at hello@meridianglow.sa with the subject "Privacy Request".
