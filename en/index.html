Frequently Asked Questions

01What is NayVella?

NayVella is a specialized beauty and personal care platform bringing together products, fragrances, clinics, doctors, and beauty professionals. The customer experience is supported by tools such as AI-assisted skin analysis, virtual try-on, and beauty routine planning.

02Is NayVella available now?

NayVella continues its development, integration, and operational testing in preparation for launch. The launch date and service availability will be announced through NayVella’s official channels once the required readiness criteria are met.

03Who can join NayVella?

NayVella serves customers, merchants, brands, clinics, doctors, beauty experts, and makeup artists, with a dedicated experience and specific registration and approval requirements for each category.

04Is NayVella only for customers?

No. NayVella is a multi-role platform with dedicated journeys for customers, merchants, clinics, doctors, beauty experts, and makeup artists.

05How can partners register their interest?

Each partner category can register its interest through its dedicated page. Applications are reviewed against the relevant registration and approval requirements before account activation.

06Can clinics and doctors join?

Yes. Eligible and licensed clinics and doctors may apply to join, subject to verification of facility information and professional licensing before activation.

07Does the skin analysis provide a medical diagnosis?

No. NayVella’s skin analysis and smart tools are designed to provide non-diagnostic information and recommendations that support understanding and decision-making. They do not replace medical advice or the judgment of a licensed professional.

08Who owns NayVella?

NayVella is a platform and trademark owned and operated by Meridian Glow Company.

About NayVella

Beauty Intelligence, Expertise and Care, Connected

NayVella is a specialized platform bringing together beauty, fragrance, and personal care products with services from clinics, doctors, beauty experts, and makeup artists. The experience is designed to help customers discover, compare, build routines, purchase products, and book services with greater clarity.

01Is NayVella a conventional online store?

No. NayVella is a multi-sided marketplace that brings independent merchants, clinics, doctors, and beauty professionals together on one platform. Each partner remains responsible for the products or services they provide in accordance with their role and obligations.

02How do smart tools support the NayVella experience?

Smart tools help customers understand skin needs, explore products, organize beauty routines, and support their choices. They are designed as supporting tools and do not replace professional or medical assessment when needed.

03Who is behind NayVella?
