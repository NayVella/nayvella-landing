Trust and Operating Register

A concise reference describing NayVella’s planned operating approach and platform boundaries. The policies and terms published at launch remain the controlling documents.

Data Protection Approach

NayVella is being designed to limit the collection of personal data and images to what a defined service requires, with role-based access and appropriate operational logs at launch.

Lifecycle of Images and AI Data

Images and inputs may be processed for virtual try-on or AI-assisted analysis after the purpose is disclosed and the appropriate consent is obtained. Retention periods and authorized access categories will be stated in the policy applicable to each service, followed by destruction or de-identification when the legal and operational need ends.

Partner Verification

Merchant, clinic, doctor, beauty-expert, and makeup-artist applications are reviewed by NayVella before activation. Payment receipt and settlement also remain subject to the payment provider’s financial verification. Each partner remains responsible for licensing, accurate information, and the products or services supplied.

Platform Responsibility Boundaries

NayVella is a marketplace and technology platform connecting parties and providing operational tools. Products and professional services are supplied by independent partners, while the approved terms and policies allocate responsibilities and define complaint, refund, and escalation processes.

AI-Tool Limitations

Virtual try-on, AI-assisted analysis, and recommendations are guidance tools and may be affected by image quality, lighting, and user inputs. They are not medical diagnoses and do not replace consultation with a licensed doctor.

Data-Subject Rights

Data subjects may submit requests for rights available under applicable law and the published policy, including access, correction, destruction, or withdrawal of consent where applicable. Identity verification may be required to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

Reporting an Account or Content

NayVella plans to provide a channel at launch to report an account, product, service, content item, or suspected misconduct. Reports will be assessed according to their nature, and interim safeguards may be applied while verification is completed.

Complaints and Contact

During the pre-launch stage, general inquiries are received at hello@meridianglow.sa and partnerships at partnerships@meridianglow.sa. Sensitive health data or images should not be sent by email unless requested through an approved secure channel.

Policy Updates and Acceptance

The platform will display current policy and terms versions and their effective dates. Material changes may require renewed acceptance from affected users, with the version, language, and acceptance time recorded in the applicable log.

Ownership and Operator

NayVella is owned and operated by Meridian Glow Company in Saudi Arabia. Meridian Glow’s official channels are used for corporate and partnership communication during the pre-launch stage.

Relationship with Clinics and Doctors

Clinics and doctors providing services through NayVella are independent entities and professionals responsible for their licensing and medical decisions. NayVella does not diagnose or make medical decisions; its role is limited to the technology, contractual, and platform-operational elements within scope.

Market and Geographic Scope

NayVella will launch first in Saudi Arabia. Future expansion into other markets remains subject to the applicable regulatory and operational requirements of each market.
